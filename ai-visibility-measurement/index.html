Measurement and improvement

How to measure search and AI visibility

There is no single universal AEO score. Track observable platform data, use consistent manual checks, and connect visibility to outcomes that matter.

Set a baseline before making changes

Choose a small set of important pages and real customer questions. Record the date, platform, query wording, landing page, impressions, clicks, qualified visits, and conversions where available. Keep branded and non-branded queries separate if that distinction matters to the business.

Combine data sources carefully

  • Google Search Console: review queries, impressions, clicks, and landing pages.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools: review indexing and search performance; its AI Performance report can expose citations and related information where available.
  • Analytics: track qualified visits and outcomes, with referral attribution configured consistently.
  • Manual sampling: check a fixed list of questions periodically; record whether a response is accurate, whether a source is cited, and whether the citation points to the right page.

AI responses vary by query, time, user context, and product. A small manual sample is directional evidence, not a complete share-of-voice measurement.

Turn observations into decisions

Compare changes over a useful period, note other changes that could explain movement, and prioritize fixes tied to reader needs or business outcomes. If impressions rise while qualified visits do not, review query intent and the page experience. If a citation is wrong, improve the source page's clarity and evidence; do not assume schema alone is the fix.
