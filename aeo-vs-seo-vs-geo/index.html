AEO vs SEO vs GEO: where they overlap

SEO, AEO, and GEO describe overlapping work around discoverability and useful information. The best approach starts with the reader and the platform evidence.

Short answer

SEO improves the ability of useful pages to be discovered in search. AEO focuses on whether a page clearly answers a specific question. GEO is a newer label for improving how a brand and its information may be understood or cited in generative search experiences. In practice, the work overlaps heavily.

Compare the work, not just the labels

  • SEO: crawlability, indexing, useful content, internal links, performance, and relevant authority.
  • AEO: clear question coverage, concise answers, sound supporting detail, and an easy-to-follow page structure.
  • GEO: accurate entity information, original and verifiable material, clear authorship, and monitoring how generative answers refer to sources.

What matters for Google AI search

Google's current guidance says the same SEO best practices remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. Pages need to meet Search technical requirements and focus on helpful, reliable, people-first content. There is no separate AEO trick that overrides these foundations.

Labels are useful for planning, but avoid creating three copies of the same page for SEO, AEO, and GEO. One strong resource can support all three when it answers a real need and is technically accessible.

Choose measurements that match the goal

For SEO, review impressions, clicks, landing pages, qualified visits, and conversions. For answer-focused work, record whether representative questions are answered accurately and whether the page is cited where the platform exposes citations. For GEO, monitor brand accuracy, source links, and relevant referrals. These metrics are imperfect; document the date, query, platform, and method so comparisons stay meaningful.
