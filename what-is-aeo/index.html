What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO is a useful way to describe making trustworthy answers easy to find and understand. It is not a shortcut that guarantees a search position or an AI citation.

A working definition

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of improving a page so that search systems and answer experiences can discover, interpret, and accurately represent its useful information. Good AEO starts with a real audience question and a direct, complete answer.

That includes familiar SEO work: crawlable pages, helpful content, clear site structure, accurate facts, and a good experience. It can also mean making definitions, steps, and supporting evidence easier for people and systems to parse.

What AEO does not promise

No markup, paragraph format, or keyword pattern can guarantee that a search engine will index a page, rank it, show a special result, or cite it in an AI response. Google says its AI search features use the same foundational SEO best practices and require pages to be eligible for ordinary Search inclusion.

Treat AEO as a content and discoverability practice. Measure whether people find and use the information; do not sell it as a guaranteed citation tactic.

A practical starting checklist

  1. Choose one clearly defined reader task or question.
  2. Answer it near the top, with the conditions that change the answer.
  3. Support material claims with reliable sources, examples, or first-hand evidence.
  4. Link to the next useful related page and make the page easy to crawl.
  5. Track search impressions, qualified visits, and useful actions over time.
