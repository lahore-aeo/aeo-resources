A simple measurement workflow

  1. Pick a small group of important pages and real customer questions.
  2. Record a baseline with dates, query wording, platform, impressions, clicks, qualified visits, and conversions where available.
  3. Repeat the same checks on a consistent schedule; label manual AI answer checks as samples.
  4. Investigate meaningful changes, note other causes, and choose one page improvement to measure next.

Keep impressions, clicks, AI citations, and business outcomes separate. Each describes a different part of visibility and none alone proves that AEO caused a result.
